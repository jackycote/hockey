## Business decisions
- Audience M11–M18; private team spaces.
- FREE: one full tactical project per team, multiple related scenarios and sequences; unlimited access to generic catalogue.
- TEAM_PRO: unlimited projects; proposed 49 CAD/season, not finalized.
- Team-specific plans and data; coaches may belong to multiple teams.
- Breakouts are first-class scenarios with contextual role models.
